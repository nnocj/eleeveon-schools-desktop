School password

Change the password used to encrypt your local school records. You must know the current password.

Existing encrypted backups still use your previous password. Keep the previous password safely if you may need those backups, and create a new backup after changing it.

2. Encrypted backups

Select a separate local folder, such as a folder mirrored by Google Drive for desktop. This does not put the live database into Google Drive.

Google Drive Desktop must be installed and signed in separately. Eleeveon saves the encrypted backup; Google Drive mirrors it when available.
