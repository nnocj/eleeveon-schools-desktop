Activate your school licence

One-time payment and licence issuance happen on the official Eleeveon website. This installation accepts only a certificate signed by Eleeveon for this exact installation.

Checking certificate...

Step 1 · Identify this Windows installation

Keep the installation ID consistent. It is stored using Windows-protected storage.

Step 2 · Purchase or activate

On Eleeveon Owner Billing, choose your Independent package, complete Paystack checkout and verify payment. Enter the identifiers above under Windows activation. Download the signed certificate once issued.

Step 3 · Import signed certificate

Choose the .json certificate downloaded from your Owner account. You do not need to expose your licence key inside this application.

Offline operation: after import, the certificate is verified locally. Signature, edition, installation and version are checked without sending school data to Eleeveon.
